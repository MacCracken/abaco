# Project-wide audit — 2026-09-30 (2.4.9)

**Scope.** The whole repository at the 2.4.8 tag plus the two 2.4.9 items the
user asked for (the `sqrt` bench-row collision and `binomial` past ganita's
limit): `src/` module by module, tests, fuzzers, benches, CI and release
workflows, scripts and docs, plus three gap probes no single area covered —
aarch64 under qemu, the bundle inside its real consumers (dhvani, jalwa), and
error-code precedence inside one expression.

**Method.** One finder agent per area (15 areas) with a written brief, each
finding then handed to an independent verifier instructed to *refute* it by
reproduction against a scratch copy of the tree; a completeness critic named
the three gaps, which got their own finder/verifier pairs. 45 agents for the
audit, then 4 for a final review of the finished diff (below).
Every fix below was made by hand afterwards, one area at a time, with tests
run after each change. Toolchain: Cyrius 6.6.12, x86_64 Linux; the aarch64
runs used `cyrius test --aarch64` under qemu-aarch64.

**Result.** 170 findings: 4 refuted, 166 confirmed — 1 critical, 14 high, 66 medium, 74 low, 11 info.
Of the confirmed: **131 fixed in code**, **22 fixed in the docs**, 11 deferred to the roadmap with a reason, 2 recorded as informational.

---

## What mattered most

- **Memory corruption in the DSP batch ops.** Every `batch_*` op handed `n` to
  the two-lane `f64v_*` intrinsics, which process pairs, so an odd `n` read
  past `src` and wrote one f64 past `dst` — heap corruption with no error.
  `batch_scale` / `batch_mac` also leaked `n * 8` bytes per call. Neither live
  consumer calls a `batch_` function, which is why nothing visible broke.
- **Units resolved the wrong unit, silently.** Every symbol was also stored
  lowercased, so `MW` and `mW` shared the key `mw` (milliwatt won) and `kN` /
  `kn` shared `kn`. `nl_parse` lowercased its whole input, so *every* natural-
  language conversion took that path: "convert 5 MW to kW" answered 0.000005.
  Unregistered symbols landed on registered neighbours (`mHz` → MHz, `Mb` →
  MB), and the plural strip made `ms` a metre. The US/imperial factors were
  6-digit truncations (1 cup = 15.999946 tbsp).
- **Integer number theory never returned near 2⁶³.** `d * d <= n` wraps once
  d passes 3037000499, so `factor` / `totient` of a prime near 2⁶³ looped for
  ~2⁶² steps; `next_prime` past the last i64 prime walked off the end.
- **The evaluator dropped what it did not understand.** Unknown bytes were
  skipped (`−5+3` with U+2212 read as 8; `[2+3]*2` as 8), malformed literals
  were accepted (`1.2.3` = 1.23), fractions were truncated by integer
  functions (`3.9!` = 6), and `eval_partial` retried once per token — O(T²),
  88 s on a 1000-token input. Each evaluation leaked 16-33 KB.
- **Tests and fuzzers that could not fail.** Several gates passed with the
  code they guard deleted: the DSP helpers accepted NaN as "near 0", the
  units fuzz identity check never looked at a factor, the fuzz rate oracle
  called the function under test, the percent-of injection test never
  reached the needle, the paren-depth test was satisfied by the token cap.
  Each was rewritten and re-run against the 2.4.8 sources (below).

## Deferred, with reasons

- **gap-aarch64-cross-target-hyperbolic-cancellation** (medium) — sinh/tanh/asinh/atanh near 0, acosh near 1 and asin near ±1 lose up to ~8 digits (both targets). *ganita upstream.*
- **gap-aarch64-cross-target-mulmod-wide-perf** (medium) — is_prime/nextprime/prevprime/mod_pow are ~300x slower on aarch64: bayan's aarch64 mulmod is always bit-serial. *bayan upstream.*
- **gap-aarch64-cross-target-no-aarch64-gate** (medium) — No CI or release gate builds or tests aarch64 (docs say CI does), so the red aarch64 suite ships. *roadmap (aarch64 CI lane).*
- **eval-functions-atan2-nan-signed-zero-inf** (medium) — atan2 turns NaN into -pi/2 and misses the C99 signed-zero and (inf, inf) rows. *NaN fixed (NaN in, NaN out); signed-zero and (Inf, Inf) rows are ganita upstream.*
- **eval-functions-hyperbolic-cancellation** (medium) — sinh/tanh/asinh/atanh lose up to 3.7e7 ulp for small |x|, acosh near 1 and asin near +-1 likewise. *ganita upstream; roadmap.*
- **eval-parse-literal-double-rounding** (medium) — Literals in the subnormal range are double-rounded and digits past 18 are dropped without a sticky bit: 2.4703282292062328e-324 parses to 0. *within the documented 1 ulp; claims corrected; roadmap "literal rounding residual".*
- **eval-functions-factorial-rounding** (low) — factorial(n) accumulates up to 6 ulp; 28! is not the nearest double. *170! pinned within 8 ulp; roadmap.*
- **eval-functions-stats-robustness** (low) — mean/stddev overflow on large finite inputs, median is NaN-order-dependent, and stdev is undocumented population stddev. *median NaN fixed; mean/stddev overflow and stdev semantics on the roadmap.*
- **dsp-windows-not-bit-symmetric** (info) — Windows and sinc_kernel are not bit-for-bit symmetric (off by up to a few ulp). *~3 ulp; centred evaluation on the roadmap.*
- **eval-functions-arity-error-code** (info) — Wrong argument count reports ABACO_ERR_UNKNOWN_FN for known functions. *roadmap (dedicated arity error).*
- **perf-dispatch-linear-streq** (info) — Function dispatch is a linear chain of up to about 45 streq calls (each with two strlen): about 1.1 us of a 1.7 us call. *measured at ~1.1 µs of a 1.7 µs call; a hashed or first-byte dispatch is a refactor of its own, on the roadmap.*

All of these are on [`docs/development/roadmap.md`](../development/roadmap.md) "Still open".

## Refuted

- **ci-11** — Release integrity: SHA256SUMS is unsigned, made by the same job, and the shipped bundle is regenerated without being compared to the committed one. Verifier: The concrete defect claimed, shipping a regenerated bundle that differs from the committed one, is prevented by the required CI gate on the same commit.
- **core-fraction-zero-den** — Value_fraction accepts a zero denominator, and Value_to_latex renders it as a valid-looking \frac{1}{0} even though Value_as_f64 rejects the same value. Verifier: Value_fraction is a raw constructor documented as 'the exact fraction num/den'.
- **core-latex-small-fixed-precision** — Floats in [1e-6, ~1e-3) render in fixed notation with only 1-3 significant digits, e.g. 1.5e-6 -> "0.000002" (33% off), while 9.9e-7 gets 7 significant digits. Verifier: This is the documented design, not a defect.
- **eval-parse-percent-implicit-mul-lost** — Port dropped the 'percent followed by a group or identifier' implicit-multiplication rule: 15%(200) now evaluates as 15 mod 200. Verifier: The Cyrius rule is consistent: '%' followed by a token that starts an operand is binary modulo, and an explicit '*' still gives percent-of.
